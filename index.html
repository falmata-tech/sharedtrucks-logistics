East Africa’s small businesses take real risks to grow, manufacture and make goods locally. They deserve a better way to reach customers. SharedTrucks uses technology to connect their shipments with reliable transport providers and available truck space, so compatible loads can move together and share the cost.

Request a quote How sharing works

Your shipment deserves
a place on the truck.

Our shared truckload model brings compatible shipments from different businesses together along similar routes and schedules. Instead of requiring every small producer to fill an entire vehicle, we use shipment and capacity information to identify opportunities to share a journey.

More affordable freight for SMEs.

A workshop, farm or growing manufacturer can share transport costs with other compatible loads rather than carrying the expense of unused truck space.

More productive journeys for carriers.

Transport providers can put available capacity to work on outbound and return journeys, creating earning opportunities from space that might otherwise go unused.

Less waste. A lighter footprint.

Using more capacity on journeys already being made can help avoid unnecessary vehicle trips, fuel consumption and associated emissions.

Technology that connects
loads and capacity.

Our technology brings shipment requirements, available vehicle capacity, planned routes and timing into a clearer view, helping identify practical shared truckload opportunities.

We review cargo compatibility and coordinate with suitable transport providers. The goal is to make existing journeys work harder while giving smaller businesses a dependable route to market.

Local production deserves
regional reach.

Every farmer who expands production, maker who opens a workshop and entrepreneur who invests in manufacturing is betting on a stronger local economy. Reaching the next market should not depend on having enough goods to fill a whole truck.

When fuel costs rise, pressure spreads from freight charges to business margins and the prices consumers pay. Making better use of trucks already travelling those routes can help reduce wasted capacity, unnecessary fuel use and the cost of moving locally made goods.

Strong local manufacturing needs more than production capacity. It needs affordable routes to market.
