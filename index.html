Request a quote How sharing works

One vehicle.
Several businesses.

Shipments that fit the same corridor and schedule can travel together. We check cargo compatibility and coordinate pickups and deliveries without moving goods through a consolidation terminal.

Lower costs for smaller loads.

Small and medium businesses share transport costs with other shippers on the same corridor. A smaller order can move without taking on the cost of an entire vehicle.

Better use of each trip.

Transport providers fill available space on outbound and return journeys. Better matched loads help reduce empty running and make each trip more productive.
